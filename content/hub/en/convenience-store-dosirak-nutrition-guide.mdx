A dosirak (도시락) is a Korean convenience store lunch box — rice plus multiple side dishes in a single tray, priced between 3,500 and 7,000 KRW. The front of the packaging shows the calorie count, but the nutrition label on the back contains the numbers that matter more for balancing a meal. This guide covers how to read that label, what calorie ranges mean in practice, and how the side dish composition affects nutritional balance.

## — What to check on the label

![Nutrition facts label on the back of a Korean convenience store dosirak — calories, sodium, carbohydrates, and protein figures on a real product packaging](/images/hub/convenience-store-dosirak-nutrition-guide/korean-convenience-store-lunch-box-nutrition-facts-label-1-en.webp)

The nutrition facts panel on the back of a dosirak lists values per serving (the full box counts as one serving). Four numbers are worth checking:

- **Calories (열량)**: The standard range for a full meal is 500–700kcal. Most convenience store dosirak fall between 400 and 800kcal
- **Sodium (나트륨)**: The daily recommended limit is 2,000mg. A single meal target is 600–700mg. Many dosirak exceed 1,000mg — this is the number most likely to be out of range
- **Carbohydrates (탄수화물)**: White rice-based dosirak are carbohydrate-heavy. Over 80g of total carbohydrates indicates a high rice-to-side-dish ratio
- **Protein (단백질)**: 10g or more indicates a reasonably substantial side dish composition. Dosirak with egg, chicken, or tofu sides tend to score higher here

If the label feels too detailed to parse quickly, a shortcut: count the side dishes. One protein-based side and one vegetable-based side is a reasonable minimum for a balanced meal.

## — Calorie ranges in practice

![Three Korean convenience store dosirak in the 400–500, 500–700, and 700–900kcal ranges placed side by side — portion size difference visible across calorie tiers](/images/hub/convenience-store-dosirak-nutrition-guide/korean-convenience-store-lunch-box-calorie-comparison-2-en.webp)

**400–500kcal**
Smaller rice portions with side dish-focused compositions. Often not sufficient as a standalone meal — best paired with another item. Some diet-labelled products fall in this range.

**500–700kcal**
The standard range. Rice and side dishes in balanced proportion, designed as a complete single meal. Price range 3,500–5,500 KRW.

**700–900kcal**
Higher rice volumes or fried / sauced side dishes. Bibimbap-style, rice bowl formats, and fried chicken side dish versions tend to land here. More suitable when physical activity is higher or as a lunch rather than a light dinner.

## — What the side dish composition tells you

![High-protein dosirak with egg roll, braised chicken, and tofu next to a kimchi and sausage version — sodium and protein difference across two side dish compositions](/images/hub/convenience-store-dosirak-nutrition-guide/korean-convenience-store-dosirak-banchan-comparison-3-en.webp)

Same calorie count, different nutritional profiles — the side dish composition is where the difference sits.

**Higher protein composition**
Egg roll (계란말이) + braised chicken + tofu side. Protein content is often 15g or above. The combination keeps hunger at bay longer relative to an equivalent calorie count from a rice-heavy dosirak.

**Higher sodium composition**
Kimchi + sausage + heavily sauced sides. Salt and soy sauce-based seasonings compound across multiple sides. Sodium can exceed 1,200mg — pairing with water rather than a sodium-containing drink is worth noting.

**Lower glycaemic index options**
Some dosirak use multigrain rice (잡곡밥) or brown rice (현미밥) instead of white rice. GS25 and CU both carry multigrain rice lines. The flavour difference is minor; the rice texture is slightly firmer.

## — GS25 and CU dosirak lines

![GS25 YOUR:S and CU HEYROO dosirak packaging placed side by side — standard and premium line side dish count comparison](/images/hub/convenience-store-dosirak-nutrition-guide/gs25-you-us-cu-heyroo-korean-dosirak-lineup-4-en.webp)

Both chains run a standard line and a premium line.

- **GS25 YOUR:S**: Standard line 3,500–4,500 KRW, premium line 5,000–7,000 KRW. Premium versions typically include six or more side dishes
- **CU HEYROO**: Similar price structure. Seasonal and collaboration dosirak appear regularly alongside the standard lineup
- **Both chains**: In-store microwaves are available. Heating time is printed on the packaging — average two to three minutes

---

**Quick Summary**

- Label check order: sodium (target under 700mg) → calories (500–700kcal standard) → protein (10g+)
- Calorie ranges: 400–500kcal (light) / 500–700kcal (standard) / 700–900kcal (substantial)
- Higher protein picks: dosirak with egg roll, braised chicken, or tofu sides
- Microwave time: two to three minutes — follow the time printed on the packaging
