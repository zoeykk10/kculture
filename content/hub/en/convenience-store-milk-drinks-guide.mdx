Banana milk is the most visible product in Korean convenience store refrigerated sections — its distinctive round bottle and yellow colour make it easy to spot. But the flavoured milk category extends well beyond banana. Korean convenience stores carry a range of processed milk drinks with distinct flavour profiles that are worth exploring once the first choice has been made. This guide covers five options, what each tastes like, and how to choose between them.

## — How the Korean Flavoured Milk Category Works

Korean convenience store milk drinks split into two broad types: premium fresh milk products focused on high cream content, and flavoured processed milk drinks. This guide covers the flavoured category.

A few things apply across all five options:

- **Volume**: Most flavoured milk drinks at convenience stores are sold in 200mL portions — a single-serving size designed to be consumed immediately
- **Calories**: Typically 100–180kcal per 200mL depending on the product and flavour
- **Storage**: All require refrigeration. Flavour is best directly from the cold shelf

## — Fruit Flavours: Strawberry, Melon, Peach

Fruit-flavoured milks are the closest in profile to banana milk and the most accessible starting point for further exploration.

**Strawberry milk**

The most recognisable after banana. Bright pink in colour, with a clear strawberry flavour and noticeable sweetness. Slightly more tart than banana milk — the fruit flavour is more forward. A natural second step after banana milk for visitors working through the flavoured milk section.

**Melon milk**

Pale green, with a soft, sweet melon aroma that is more fragrant than banana. Sweet but lighter. Works particularly well cold, and is one of the more refreshing options in warm weather. Melon milk can sell out at some stores depending on season — worth buying on sight rather than returning for it later.

**Peach milk**

The most restrained of the three fruit options. Soft peach fragrance, light sweetness, and the milk flavour comes through more clearly than in strawberry or melon. A good choice for people who find the other fruit milks too sweet.

## — Richer Flavours: Black Sesame, Chocolate, Coffee

For something further from banana milk in character, the richer flavour options offer a different experience entirely.

**Black sesame milk (흑임자우유)**

Made from black sesame (흑임자), a traditional Korean ingredient used in porridge and rice cakes. The flavour is nutty, slightly earthy, and less sweet than the fruit options. Unfamiliar on the first sip for most international visitors, but consistently well-regarded on return. Among the five options here, this is the most distinctly Korean in character.

**Chocolate milk**

The most immediately accessible option for visitors unfamiliar with Korean flavoured milk. Sweet chocolate flavour balanced with milk — similar in profile to chocolate milk elsewhere, but with a higher milk content that makes the texture slightly richer. A reliable choice if black sesame sounds too adventurous as a starting point.

**Coffee milk**

Lightly coffee-flavoured milk with a small amount of caffeine. Sweetened, closer to an iced café latte than a strong coffee drink. Popular as a light morning option and frequently bought alongside a triangle kimbap or rice ball as a quick breakfast combination.

## — What to Check Before Buying

The same flavour name from different brands can taste noticeably different. Two label checks narrow the choice.

**Raw milk content (원유 함량)**

Some products display the percentage of raw milk on the front of the packaging. Higher raw milk content means a richer, creamier texture and more pronounced milk flavour — and a slightly higher price.

**Sugar content (당류)**

For visitors monitoring sugar intake, the nutrition panel on the back lists sugar content per serving. Fruit milk drinks tend to run higher in sugar; black sesame and coffee milk are generally lower. The Korean term to look for is 당류.

**Price range**

Standard flavoured milk drinks at convenience stores are priced between 1,300 and 2,000 KRW. Premium products using higher-grade milk are stocked separately and run 2,000–3,000 KRW.

**Quick Summary**

- Fruit options: strawberry (tart, pink) / melon (sweet, fragrant) / peach (mild, light)
- Richer options: black sesame (nutty, distinctly Korean) / chocolate (sweet, familiar) / coffee (light caffeine, latte-style)
- Starting order: strawberry or chocolate → melon or peach → black sesame
- Most are 200mL, priced 1,300–2,000 KRW
- Higher raw milk content = richer texture and higher price
